## 1. Ownership settled by the native-L72 control (the decisive new diagnostic)

`tests/diag_l72_complex_control.py` runs the EXACT ring-osc + opamp gate circuits
through PyCircuitSim's own solver with the ground-truth BSIM-CMG (LEVEL=72) OSDI
model (no NN) vs NGSPICE. Recommended in plan V6.5.2.4, never built until now.

| Gate | native-L72-in-PyCircuitSim vs NGSPICE | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| ring-osc | period err **0.00%** (tsmc5/7), <0.8% (tsmc12/16) | gate well-posed; solver faithful |
| opamp (continuation-first) | gain err **0.00–0.10%** all 4 techs | gate well-posed; solver finds the high-gain basin |

⇒ The 2ps fixed timestep is adequate (refutes "enable substeps / finer dt" as a
fix for the NN ring gap); the opamp DC sweep finds gain≈188 with ground truth via
the S2 continuation-first path. Both remaining gaps are the SAME class: **the NN
under-predicts drive/gm in the switching / high-gain region for the steep
low-VDD techs (tsmc5/7)** — value-surface-owned, not solver/harness. (Side note:
the solver has only multiplicative LM/adaptive damping and NO SPICE
junction-voltage limiting, so the legacy source-stepping DC path diverges on the
opamp; continuation-first masks it. Latent robustness item, not gate-blocking.)

## 2. Scaffold audit (7-agent workflow) — no gate-changer; 3 hygiene fixes

Token-by-token diff of every rendered NN deck vs its NGSPICE deck. **No
V6.5.3-class gate-changing bug.** Three LOW latent inconsistencies fixed, all

## 3. Why a fresh retrain (the production-regression finding + the bake-off probe)

The installed clean-recipe `tsmc{X}_dn_medium` (V6.4.9/V6.5 benchmark retrain)
had REGRESSED the value-surface gates vs the V6.4.7 ship. A zero-training
diagnostic bake-off over the on-disk checkpoint pool confirmed the gaps were
*which-checkpoint-fits-the-OP-best* and CIRCUIT-DEPENDENT (tsmc5-large recovers
opamp but worsens ring). Rather than ship a heterogeneous mix of **stale**
checkpoints, the decision was to **delete all stale checkpoints and retrain the
full matrix from scratch on freshly regenerated data**, then select the best
config per tech. (The bake-off scripts are retired; its lesson — per-tech config
selection — carried into the retrain.)

## 4. The fresh retrain (executed)

1. **Data regen** — `scripts/benchmark_gen_data.sh`: all 8 `{tech}_{dev}.npz`
   rebuilt from PyCMG (grid + inv-trip + subvt-off overlays, `--variants all`),
   2.0–2.5M samples each, 0 bins dropped.
2. **Hard-deleted all 436 stale checkpoint files** (the entire on-disk pool:
   clean-recipe S/M/L/XL, pivcor/s12cor/ctlv2/c17/lg_s*, killed-lever artifacts).
3. **Trained 32 fresh models** — `scripts/benchmark_train_sml.sh --force`,
   S/M/L/XL × 4 techs × 2 devices, ONE uniform clean recipe
   (`--apply-filter off --swa-mode ema --seed 42`), 9 concurrent across 3×RTX4090.
   Val losses textbook (tsmc5: small 8.3e-3 → medium 1.0e-3 → large 2.6e-4 →
   xl 2.1e-4; XL tightest = the known over-fit tier).
4. **Per-size eval** (`scripts/v6_5_4_eval_sizes.py`) → best size per tech = **13/16**
   (tsmc5/7/12 large, tsmc16 medium; only tsmc12 4/4).
5. **Stage-B seed sweep** (`scripts/v6_5_4_seed_sweep.sh`) at `large` for the 3
   open-gate techs × seeds {7,17,31} — the documented per-tech value-surface
   lever. Final selection (`scripts/v6_5_4_eval_seeds.py`) → **14/16**.

## 6. The 2 residual gates — genuine value-surface limits on clean data

- **tsmc5 ring-osc (12.66%)** — fails at every size AND every seed on clean data
  (best clean seed: lgs17 9.64%, still > 5%). Lowest-VDD, steepest curves; the NN
  inverter is too slow at the switching edge.
- **tsmc7 opamp (gain→0)** — no size and no clean-data seed at large recovers the
  high-gain basin (lgs7/17/31 all collapse).

Both were only cleared by the V6.4.7 ship via **corridor-augmented training data**
(pivcor "pivot-corridor" for tsmc7, s12cor for the opamp basin) — i.e. densifying
the diff-pair / inverter-switching OP locus, NOT a clean-grid lever. That is the
single remaining lever (Tier-2, A/B-gated, requires regenerating the corridor
overlay). Deferred pending a go-ahead, since it reintroduces a non-clean data
recipe for 2 of 16 gates.
