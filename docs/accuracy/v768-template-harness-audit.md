# V7.6.8 template and harness audit

Date: 2026-09-03

Audited baseline: `12c7769` (`V7.6.8`); fixes are in this follow-up commit.

Status: implementation hardening; no diagnostic was promoted and the frozen
`simple-v1` `/20` denominator is unchanged.

## Outcome

The template inventory and harness are internally consistent after review:

- 44 authoritative templates: 1 control, 1 L0, 4 L1, 10 L2, 15 L3, 4 L4,
  and 9 subcircuit fixtures;
- 4 frozen `simple-v1` cases and 29 held-out `simple-v2` cases;
- 75 `simple-v2` analyses across OP, DC, transient, and AC;
- no duplicate physical experiment when rendered topology, physical values,
  analysis card, signals, and metric profile are compared together.

The review found failures that the original unit suite did not exercise. The
following were corrected:

1. The unified CLI crashed when printing a valid derived CMRR/PSRR row because
   that row intentionally has no trace-level NRMSE.
2. All four common-source AC diagnostics required a finite bandwidth but had
   no explicit output capacitance; their reference response did not cross
   -3 dB. A 10 fF load now puts the declared pole inside the sweep.
3. The geometry guard crashed on unsupported TSMC7 VT/L corners and omitted
   role-specific `nfin_delta` coordinates. It now follows catalog
   applicability and includes the actual NFIN=4/6 role points.
4. PMOS subthreshold metrics read the numerically first (on-state) point as
   `Ioff`, which also made subthreshold slope null. Both polarities are now
   ordered by increasing `|Vgs|`, and every promised device metric must be
   finite.
5. Explicit `execution_state="complete"` could override partial/nonconverged
   flags. Structured campaign rows could also omit provenance, disagree with
   their completion code or family tag, or carry no numeric evidence. These
   paths now fail closed; exit 2 is not a scientific verdict.
6. DC/transient traces could contain arbitrarily large internal gaps while
   satisfying only their endpoints. Trace and device-sweep validation now
   enforces monotonicity and the declared maximum output step.
7. Device and terminal suites checked connectivity but not model family,
   L/NFIN, VT, analysis card, or modelcard binding. They now use the complete
   physical-deck parity contract before simulation.
8. The LEVEL=72 opamp control imported a removed bias helper, and the Jacobian
   diagnostic sent universal technology IDs into local-vocabulary checkpoints.
   Both standalone diagnostics now execute with current shared contracts.
9. The parametric circuit sweep returned 0 with explicit `ERROR` rows. It now
   returns 2 for any uncharacterized requested row and 1 for numeric misses.
10. A failed fast DC operating point could enter the transient after an
    unconverged GMIN retry. Both attempts now require a physical fixed point.
11. Device AC, opamp AC, and the two NN parametric CLIs used regex-only campaign
    evidence. They now emit exact, provenance-bound denominators; incomplete or
    stale JSON/log rows do not count as coverage.
12. Device and opamp AC used a different source bias for each engine. LEVEL=72
    now locates one physical bias used by both adapters, with full deck parity,
    DC/AC axis validation, and MRE/R²/NRMSE/maximum-error evidence.
13. Flat-versus-nested NN execution was behavior-checked without comparing the
    expanded hierarchy. Parsed components, geometry, sources, models, analysis,
    and initial conditions are now compared before simulation. LEVEL=72 control
    parity likewise checks model bindings and L/NFIN/TFIN, not topology alone.
14. The legacy circuit sweep could aggregate partial ring/switch-cap prefixes,
    accept incomplete opamp/SRAM DC axes, and drop every corner after a baseline
    miss. It now runs the complete denominator and fails closed on incomplete or
    undefined events.

## Redundancy decisions

- Removed the `mos_ratio_reference` campaign case. Its high-impedance supply
  sweep and line-sensitivity metrics now belong to `diode_load/load_high`, as
  the V7.6.8 changelog intended.
- Replaced the Python-generated fanout block with explicit 3T, 5T, 9T, and 17T
  templates. They are distinct scale topologies, not duplicate analyses.
- Replaced programmatic ring expansion with explicit 3-, 5-, 7-, and 9-stage
  templates while preserving the frozen 5-stage rendered decks byte-for-byte.
- Moved `diffpair_active_load` to L3 because its mirror gate is generated by
  devices. Its element topology now lives in the template rather than a Python
  substitution.
- Retained the `simple-v1`/`simple-v2` reuse of the opamp, ring, and
  switched-capacitor templates. The analyses answer different questions, and
  merging them would mutate the frozen qualification denominator.
- Retained both SRAM states/write directions, transmission-gate directions,
  lifted-source terminal points, OP-versus-ramp cells, and flat-versus-nested
  execution. Each distinguishes a polarity, solver path, frame, or hierarchy
  contract and is therefore not redundant.

## Coverage still not claimed

The repository now detects these boundaries rather than implying they are
covered:

- `simple-v2` remains diagnostic: thresholds and three-repeat LEVEL=72
  stability are not frozen.
- The exhaustive five-technology, all-corner, four-family numerical campaign
  was not rerun here. All applicable decks were rendered and parity-checked;
  the executable smoke used the pinned TSMC12 LEVEL=75 family.
- Historical device-AC `/10` and opamp-AC `/5` totals use the retired
  per-engine-bias definition. They are not current-gate evidence until the
  corrected shared-bias gates are rerun across all five technologies.
- Terminal-current/capacitance diagnostics remain nominal-geometry tests.
  PVT/L/NFIN full-terminal capacitance coverage is still absent.
- The generated-bias scale/cascode diagnostics are NMOS-led. There is no
  complementary PMOS-only self-bias ladder.
- NN hierarchy is exercised dynamically through a nested inverter buffer.
  Broader NN subcircuit DC and AC coverage is absent.
- `.noise`, `.pz`, `.disto`, `.sens`, and controlled-source topologies remain
  out of scope because the parser does not support those analyses/elements.

The isolated catalog-wide TSMC12 LEVEL=75 nominal pass produced all 78 expected
rows across all 29 case IDs: 73 characterized and converged, with five explicit
candidate errors. The errors were `nand2/input_b` just outside certified model
support, both `sram6t_modes` write-margin directions at the same unconverged
0.22 V point, and `beta_multiplier` up/down continuation failures at 0 V and
0.19 V. All four explicit fanout cases were present. These rows retain their
denominator slots and do not enter numeric aggregates.

The corrected open-loop opamp AC gate returned an 8.91 dB numeric model miss.
The opamp VCM parametric smoke retained one explicit out-of-support row. These
are scientific/coverage outcomes, not harness crashes.
